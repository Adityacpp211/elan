Élan helps people in a cardiac emergency alert nearby hospitals with their location and symptoms, and lets hospital staff respond. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you control it. It applies to the Élan Android app and to the Élan service the app connects to.

Élan is not an emergency service. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 (or 108 for an ambulance) first. Élan notifies hospitals but cannot guarantee that anyone responds.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address, passwordWhen you create an account To identify you and keep your account secure. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash; we can never see it.
Phone number (optional)If you add it to your profile So a hospital responding to your alert can call you.
Precise locationOnly while the Emergency screen is open, and when you send an alertTo show hospitals near you and to tell the hospitals you alert where to find you. We do not track your location in the background.
Health information: symptoms and the message you writeWhen you send an alertSo the hospital knows what kind of help is needed.
Health records you choose to enter (patients, vital signs, reports) Only if you use those sections of the appTo store them for you.
Alert historyWhen you send an alertWhich hospitals were notified, their responses and estimated arrival times, so you can follow your alert.
Hospital staff detailsIf you register as hospital staffYour hospital, and which alerts you accepted or passed on, so patients and your hospital can see who responded.
Payment recordsOnly if paid alerts are enabledAmount, date and payment ID. Card and bank details are handled by our payment provider (Razorpay) and never reach us.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data. Your IP address is processed briefly to protect the service from abuse (for example, rate limiting) and is not stored with your account.

Who can see your information

  • The hospitals you alert receive your name, phone number (if provided), location, symptoms and message, by in-app alert and by email to their emergency inbox.
  • Hospital staff who respond are recorded against your alert, and you see which hospital responded and its estimated arrival time.
  • Service providers who run parts of Élan for us, only as needed: our hosting provider, our email delivery provider, Google Firebase (for notifications, if enabled) and Razorpay (for payments, if enabled).
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and alerts until you delete your account. When you do, we permanently delete your account, alerts (including each hospital's copy), location, and any health records you entered. Payment records, if any, are kept for accounting and tax obligations without your name, email or alert details.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete: in the app under Profile → Delete account, or on the web at /delete-account.
  • Location: you can withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's settings. Élan then cannot show nearby hospitals or send your location.
  • Questions or complaints: contact us at the address below. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also have the right to a summary of your data, to nominate someone to act for you, and to escalate a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and hospital staff accounts must be approved before they can see any alerts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Élan accounts are for adults (18 and over). If a child needs help, an adult can send an alert. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
