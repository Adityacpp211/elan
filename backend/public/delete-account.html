Delete your Élan account

You can also do this in the app: Profile → Delete account. Use this page if you no longer have the app installed.

What is deleted, permanently

  • Your account: name, email, phone number and password
  • Your emergency alerts, including symptoms, messages and locations, and each hospital's copy of them
  • Your saved location and notification token
  • Any patient, vital-sign and report records you created

What is kept

  • Payment transaction records (amount, date, payment ID), kept for accounting and tax obligations. They are no longer linked to your name, email or alerts.

Confirm it's you

Deletion happens immediately. If you forgot your password, contact the Élan support address listed in the Play Store and we will verify your identity another way.
